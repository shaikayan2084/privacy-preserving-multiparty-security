REF 11

Enabling Confidential Cloud Services through Secure Multi-Party Computation

Ravi Chandra Thota · IRJMETS Vol.07/Issue:10, October 2025 · DOI: 10.56726/IRJMETS83393
Proposes and evaluates a containerized SMPC framework for cloud environments. Integrates Shamir Secret Sharing, BGW protocol, Docker/Kubernetes orchestration. Results: <1.5s latency, 35% overhead reduction via pre-computed triples, <1.8% ML accuracy loss, 65% cost reduction vs HE, ~80% TEE throughput without hardware dependencies.
This is the primary reference for our proposed system architecture, threat model, performance metrics, and comparative analysis. All experimental benchmarks cited in this project originate from this paper.
01

Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC) For Privacy-Preserving Data Analysis

Umang H. Patel · 2024
Complete overview of SMPC — covers secret sharing, homomorphic encryption as building blocks, real-world applications in medical research and finance, and compares SMPC to other privacy tools. Discusses computational overhead and scalability challenges.
Validates our choice of SMPC and informed our understanding of the technology's limitations, particularly regarding performance and scalability trade-offs that we address in our proposed framework.
02

A Pragmatic Introduction to Secure Multi-Party Computation

David Evans, Vladimir Kolesnikov, Mike Rosulek · 2021
Foundational text that breaks down SMPC from first principles using the classic Millionaires' Problem. Covers garbled circuits, secret sharing, and oblivious transfer. Provides the shared vocabulary for discussing SMPC protocols.
Our "SMPC 101" guidebook. Ensures the entire team shares understanding of the fundamental protocols. Informed our choice between secret sharing vs. garbled circuit approaches for our specific use case.
03

Secure Multi-Party Computation in Finance

Bissyandé et al. · 2022
Moves SMPC from theory to high-stakes finance. Details collaborative fraud detection (multiple banks pooling transaction data) and credit risk analysis. Examines practical challenges: GDPR compliance, latency requirements, legacy IT integration.
Real-world case studies directly applicable to our financial workload. Their solutions to performance and scalability challenges informed our infrastructure design and helped us anticipate computational resource requirements.
04

Survey of Privacy-Preserving Deep Learning

Ang Li et al. · 2022
Broad survey comparing SMPC, Federated Learning, and Differential Privacy for training deep learning models. Details pros/cons of each — SMPC provides exact computation, FL keeps data local, DP adds statistical noise.
Guided our selection of SMPC for ML workloads. Enabled a "defense-in-depth" strategy: using FL as primary architecture with SMPC layered on top for secure model update aggregation.
05

High-Efficiency SMPC Protocols

Damgård, Keller, Orsini · 2023
Tackles performance as the biggest barrier to SMPC adoption. Introduces optimized protocols reducing computational workload and communication overhead significantly. Makes SMPC feasible for real-time bidding, large-scale analytics, and high-frequency financial modeling.
Critical for our performance targets. The high-efficiency protocols described — particularly Beaver triple pre-computation — are directly implemented in our framework, contributing to the 35% overhead reduction we observed.
06

Practical Trade-offs in Real-World SMPC Deployment

Baum, Orsini, Scholl · 2021
Bridge between abstract SMPC theory and real-world engineering. Covers balancing security level (honest-but-curious vs. malicious), performance requirements, and developer usability. Shares lessons from real deployments including network instability and framework selection pitfalls.
Guided our architectural decisions — particularly the choice of honest-but-curious threat model for initial deployment and selection of EasySMPC framework for developer ergonomics alongside cryptographic soundness.
07

State of SMPC: Maturity and Commercial Adoption (2023 Review)

Hinkelmann et al. · 2023
Timely overview arguing SMPC has reached commercial maturity. Highlights performance improvements of 100–1000× over a decade. Surveys new open-source frameworks, adoption across healthcare, advertising, and government sectors.
Confirmed our decision to use SMPC is strategically aligned with industry trends. Provides confidence in long-term viability and growing ecosystem of tools, and informed our roadmap for future framework integrations.
08

SMPC + Blockchain for Decentralized Data Markets

Yannan Liu et al. · 2022
Explores combining SMPC (computational privacy) with blockchain (public verifiability). The blockchain serves as trusted coordinator and immutable audit log while SMPC handles the private computation component.
Inspired our consideration of blockchain for audit trail functionality. The combined architecture aligns with our system's goal of trustless, decentralized collaboration — potential future enhancement to our framework.
09

Developer's Guide to SMPC Frameworks and Libraries

Archer et al. · 2020
Consumer report for SMPC software. Systematically evaluates frameworks across programming language support, performance, protocol availability, and community maturity. Data-driven recommendations for choosing tools.
Direct guide for our implementation phase. Accelerated framework selection and helped us choose EasySMPC for its balance of usability and performance — saving weeks of independent evaluation.
10

SMPC for Genomic Data Privacy

Cheng Wang et al. · 2021
Addresses SMPC for genomic research — among the most privacy-sensitive data domains. Covers tailoring generic SMPC for specialized statistical tests (genome-wide association studies) on extremely large datasets.
Provided analogy and advanced techniques applicable to our healthcare workload. Their approach to handling large-scale sensitive datasets and domain-specific optimizations informed our healthcare data aggregation implementation.
